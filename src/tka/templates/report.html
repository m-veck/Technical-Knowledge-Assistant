1 Search

Did the search find the right documents?

Three ways to search the same index, scored with the three numbers FreshStack uses. Higher is better, 1.0 is perfect. The assistant uses hybrid.

2 Answer

What did the assistant do with them?

One Claude call per question, with the 10 documents and nothing else. It must answer from those documents and name its sources, or say that they do not cover the question.

Rules checked by code

3 Quality

How good are the answers?

A judge (Claude again, two calls per answer) grades every answered question. It gets the nuggets and the accepted Stack Overflow answer. The assistant never sees those.

Where do nuggets get lost?

Every nugget of an answered question, split by what the search did for it. The colours are the judge's verdict on the answer. Nuggets of questions without an answer have their own bar.

What these numbers cannot tell you
  • The labels are incomplete.Only documents that FreshStack's own search runs returned were ever labelled. A document nobody labelled can still hold the fact, so "no supporting document" does not always mean the search missed.
  • A labelled document is not a quote.The label says a document is relevant for a nugget. It does not say the document states the fact plainly for this user's case.
  • The judge is an LLM, and the same model as the assistant.On the dev split a second Claude pass (not a person) graded a sample of 45 nuggets again, without seeing the judge's verdict. Full and none held up, partial was a bit strict, so the strict nugget score is a lower bound.
  • The reference answers are older than the corpus.Some accepted Stack Overflow answers use imports that the October 2024 documents no longer use. An answer that follows the documents can miss a nugget for that reason.
